💡 INTERPRETATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OWT has HIGHER loss because:
  • More diverse vocabulary
  • More complex syntax
  • More varied topics
  • Less repetition
  • More realistic language

This doesn't mean OWT model is worse!
  • Lower loss = better fit to training distribution
  • TinyStories is simpler → easier to fit
  • OWT model is more useful for general tasks
